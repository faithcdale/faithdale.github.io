Chicken Cassoulet

Original Link

Cassoulet is a bean and meat casserole with a crispy topping that originates from Southern France. Though traditionally it uses duck confit and sausages, this version uses chicken thighs instead.

Ingredients
2 tbsp cornflour / cornstarch
1.75lb / 6 pieces boneless skinless chicken thighs
1 tsp salt
1 tsp black pepper
2 tbsp olive oil
7 oz bacon (cut into small pieces)
2 brown onions (diced)
2 carrots (peeled, cut into half-inch thick pieces)
3 garlic cloves (minced)
1 tbsp tomato paste
1/2 tsp thyme
1 tsp rosemary
2 bay leaves
1/4 cup of chardonnay or other dry white wine
1 1/2 cup chicken stock
3 14oz cans cannellini beans (drained)
12 cherry tomatoes
Topping
1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
2 tsp parsley
2 tbsp olive oil
1/4 tsp salt
  1. Preheat oven to 390 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Make the slurry. Whisk the cornflour in a small bowl with 1/4 cup of the chicken stock until lump free. Set aside.
  3. Sprinkle both sides of the chicken using 3/4 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
  4. Heat the oil in a shallow 30cm / 12" ovenproof casserole dish over medium-high heat. Cook the bacon until golden (about 4 minutes), then transfer into a small bowl using a slotted spoon, leaving all the fat in the pan.
  5. Turn the heat up to high. Add more oil if the pan is looking dry. Place the chicken into the pan, smooth side down and cook for 3 minutes until golden brown, flip then cook the other side for 1 minute. Remove onto a plate and set aside.
  6. Add the onion, garlic and carrot and cook, stirring, for 3 minutes until the onion has softened. Add the tomato paste, thyme, rosemary and bay leaves. Stir for 1 minute.
  7. Add the wine and simmer for 30 seconds, scraping the base of the dish.
  8. Add the rest of the stock, cornflour slurry (give it a quick stir to redistribute the cornflour), beans, cooked bacon and remaining salt and pepper. Stir until combined, place the chicken on top (pour over any juices pooled on the plate), smooth side up. Press the chicken down slightly. Scatter over the cherry tomatoes.
  9. Bring it to a simmer, then place into the oven for 20 minutes, uncovered.
  10. While the chicken is cooking, mix the topping ingredients in a bowl and set aside.
  11. Turn the oven grill/broiler on to 240°C / 460°F (220°C fan-forced). Pull the dish out and sprinkle the panko topping on top, avoiding the chicken and tomatoes. Place the dish back into the oven (15cm / 6" away from the grill) and leave for 2 to 3 minutes to allow the surface to brown.
