Eggnog



Eggnog is a rich, creamy, and chilled dairy-based drink that's typically made with milk or cream, sugar, egg yolks, and whipped egg whites. The whipped egg whites give eggnog its frothy texture and its name. Eggnog is often served cold and has a custard-like color and texture. It can also have a slightly spiced flavor from nutmeg and cinnamon.

Ingredients
3 cups whole milk
6 eggs
1 cup sugar
2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
1 teaspoon of ground cinnamon
1/4 teaspoons of ground cloves
1 tablespoon and 2 teaspoons of ginger powder
1 and 1/2 teaspooons of ground nutmeg
1 and 1/2 cups of heavy whipping cream
1/4 cup of rum (optional)
Topping
6 eggs whites
12 tablespoons of sugar
6 teaspoons vanila extract
  1. Measure out 3 cups of whole milk and add it to a medium sized pot.
  2. Heat up the milk to a steam, but not to a simmer.
  3. Separate the egg yolks into a bowl and whisk yolks until slightly lighter in color.
  4. While whisking, gradually add 1 cup of sugar to the mixture.
  5. When milk is hot and steamy, slowly add the milk into the bowl, while whisking the eggs.
  6. Add mixture back into the pot, and add in 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract.
  7. Cook on low heat for 10 - 15 minutes or until thickened. Keep the temperature of the mixture close to, but below, 175 degrees Fahrenheit.
  8. The longer it is being cooked, the thicker the mixture will be. Thicker mixtures are better for mixing in the gound cinnamon.
  9. Pour contents into a clean bowl through a strainer.
  10. While stirring the yolk mixture, slowly add in the spices.
  11. Add in 1/4 cup of rum and mix (optional).
  12. Add in 1 and 1/2 cups of heavy whipping cream.
  13. Put mixture in ice water bath while mixing.
  14. Store eggnog in a bowl or some glasses and let it cool in the fridge for at least two hours.
  15. Lastly, for the topping, add 6 egg whites into a clean bowl and use a hand mixer to whisk the egg whites until slightly frothly.
  16. Add in 12 tablespoons of sugar and continue to whisk until it turns creamy. This should take no more 6 minutes if using a hand mixer.
  17. Add in 6 teaspoons of vanilla extract and continue whisking until mixture is firm and the topping is done.
